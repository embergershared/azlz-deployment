# Azure Landing Zone - Deployment
Azure Landing Zone deployment wrapper repo/code to specific requirements &amp; context.

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [platform](platform/README.md) | Management & Connectivity platform subscriptions, from the [ALZ Bicep Accelerator](https://github.com/Azure/alz-bicep-accelerator) templates |
| [billing-alerts](billing-alerts/README.md) | Monthly cost budgets with email alerts per subscription |

Management groups, policies and assignments are deployed from a separate governance repository.
